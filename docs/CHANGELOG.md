# What's new

Version numbers come from the GitHub build number: build 5 is version 1.5, and so on.

## 1.8

- No more closing the app to update. Fic Nook looks for a new version every time you come back to it and every 30 minutes while it's open.
- New **Check for updates** button under **Me**.

## 1.7

- Fics in your fic folder are read again once after updating, so ones you added before 1.5 also get italics, bold and special fonts. Your spot, highlights and shelves stay. (Fics you added by hand with **Add a fic** need adding again.)

## 1.6

- Fixed the cards under **Me** sitting squashed together with no space between them.
- The **Update now** bar now also shows at the top of your Library, and the app looks for new versions again when you come back to it (before, it only checked when it first started).

## 1.5

- **Your own shelves**, like "Comfort reads". They show up as tabs in your Library. Make them from a fic's ⋮ button or under **Me**.
- **Star ratings** (1 to 5) and **private notes** on each fic. Your notes are included when you search, and you can sort by rating.
- **Bookmarks**: save a page from the reading controls, and find your bookmarks under **Chapters**.
- **Warm theme** and a **night light** that tints the screen amber, either always on or on evenings only (8 pm to 7 am), with a strength slider.
- **Auto-scroll** for hands-free reading at your reading speed, with pause, slower and faster.
- **Quote cards**: turn a highlight into a picture with the fic's title and author, in four styles, and share or save it.
- **Reading goals**: fics a week, month or year, or minutes a day, with a progress bar under **Me**.
- **Home screen widget**: "Keep reading" shows your current fic and opens it with one tap.
- **PDFs keep the author's special fonts** (like text messages and signs), plus *italics* and **bold**. Before, all of these came out as plain text.
- Text boxes in panels no longer spill past the right edge.

## 1.4

- **Simpler layout**: a three-button bar (Library, Reading, Me). The reading screen shows only the story, and tapping the middle brings up a small panel with text size, theme, read aloud and chapters. Everything else is under **More**.
- **Flip pages** by tapping the edges, swiping, or pressing the **volume buttons**. Scrolling is still available.
- **Read aloud** with the phone's voice, carrying on into the next chapter, with speed control and a **sleep timer**.
- **Shelves** (To read, Reading, Finished) as tabs, plus **favourites**.
- **Filters** by fandom, ship, rating, complete or still updating, and **hidden tags**.
- **Series** fold into one card with the parts in order, and the end of a part links to the next.
- **Reading stats** and a **yearly recap**.
- **Highlights and notes**.
- **Time left** on every fic and chapter, based on your reading speed.
- **Backups** to Google Drive (or anywhere your phone can save), and restore.
- **Re-downloaded fics update in place**: a fic with new chapters is recognised as the same fic and keeps your spot, highlights and shelf, with a "new chapters" label on its card.
- **First-time setup** (pick your folder, then light or dark) and a one-time tip on how to use the reading screen.
- Read aloud now works on newer Android versions that hide the text-to-speech service from apps.

## 1.3

- **Dark by default**, since the light look was too bright.
- **Fic folder**: pick the folder your downloads go to, and new fics appear by themselves.
- **Search** by title, author, tags and summary, with tag chips.
- **In-app updates**: the app checks for new versions and installs them with one tap. No more downloading the APK by hand.

## 1.1 and 1.2

- The first Android app: the web reader wrapped in an app, with PDF, EPUB and HTML support, themes, fonts, text size, line spacing, remembered reading spots and a shelf.
- Automatic APK builds on GitHub.

## Before the app

- Fic Nook started as a web page (a Claude artifact) that opens AO3 downloads and makes them comfortable to read on a phone.
