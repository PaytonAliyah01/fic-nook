# FicNook user guide

This guide covers everything FicNook can do as of version 1.9.

## Getting started

### Installing

See [Get the app](../README.md#get-the-app). After that, updates arrive inside the app.

### First-time setup

The first time you open FicNook it asks two things:

1. **Where do your fics go?** Pick the folder your AO3 downloads are saved in (usually **Download**). Any PDF, EPUB or HTML fic in that folder shows up in your Library by itself. You can skip this and add fics by hand instead.
2. **Light or dark?** You can change this any time.

### Getting a fic into FicNook

On AO3, open the fic, tap **Download** and pick **PDF** or **EPUB**. EPUB comes out a little cleaner, but PDF works well too.

- If you picked a fic folder, the fic appears on its own the next time you open FicNook (or tap **Check for new fics** under **Me**).
- Otherwise, tap **Add a fic** in your Library and pick the file.

### When a fic gets new chapters

Download it again from AO3. FicNook recognises it's the same fic (it uses the AO3 work number, or the title and author if that's missing), so it updates the copy you already have. Your reading spot, highlights, bookmarks, rating, notes and shelves all stay. The card shows "1 new chapter" (or however many) until you open it, and a fic you'd finished moves back to **Reading**.

## Getting around

The bar along the bottom has three buttons:

- **Library**: all your fics.
- **Reading**: jumps straight back into the fic you were reading.
- **Me**: your goal, shelves, stats, highlights, settings, fic folder, backups and help.

The Android **Back** button closes whatever is open, one step at a time.

## Your Library

Each card shows the title, author, fandom, your star rating, how far you've read and how long is left (for example "40% read · 1 h 10 min left"). A ★ before the title means it's a favourite.

- **Tabs** along the top: All, Reading, To read, Finished, Favourites, plus any shelves you made yourself.
- **Search** looks through titles, authors, tags, summaries and your private notes.
- **Sort** by last read, newest added, title, author, shortest first, or your rating.
- **Filters** (one button) narrow things down by fandom, ship, rating, and whether the fic is complete or still updating. You can also **hide fics with certain tags**, like Major Character Death. A note tells you how many fics are hidden, with a **Show all** link.
- **Series** fold into one card with the parts in order. Tap it to see the parts. At the end of a part, the reader offers the next one.

### The ⋮ button on a fic

Tap **⋮** on any card to:

- Move it between **To read**, **Reading** and **Finished**.
- Add it to your **favourites**.
- Give it a **star rating** (tap the same star again to clear it).
- Put it on **your own shelves**, or make a new shelf with **+ New shelf**.
- Write **private notes** about it (they save as you type, and only you can see them).
- Tap a **tag** to find other fics with that tag.
- **Remove** it from your library (tap twice to confirm).

## Reading

While you read, only the story is on screen.

- **Tap the middle** of the page to show or hide the controls.
- **Tap the right or left edge**, swipe, or press the **volume buttons** to turn pages (volume down goes forward).
- If you prefer scrolling, switch **Turning pages** to **Scroll** under **More**.

### The controls

When you tap the middle, a panel appears at the bottom:

- **A− / A+**: text size.
- **Theme circles**: Light, Sepia, Dark, Night (pure black) and Warm (dark brown with amber text, gentle at night).
- **Read aloud**: see below.
- **Chapters**: jump to any chapter, and see your bookmarks and highlights for this fic.
- **Bookmark**: saves the page you're on. Tap it again on the same page to remove it.
- **More**: all the other settings.

### More settings

Theme, night light, font, text size, line spacing, page width, scroll or flip pages, volume buttons, your reading speed (used for every "time left"), author's notes shown or folded away, the info page before chapter 1, straight right edge, and auto-scroll.

### Night light

Under **More**, set **Night light** to **On**, or to **Evenings** so it switches on by itself from 8 pm to 7 am. It tints the whole screen amber, which is easier on your eyes in bed. A slider sets how strong it is.

### Auto-scroll

Under **More**, tap **Start auto-scroll**. FicNook moves through the fic by itself at your reading speed: it turns pages in flip mode, or scrolls slowly in scroll mode, and carries on into the next chapter. A bar at the bottom lets you pause, go slower or faster (− and +), or stop.

### Read aloud

Tap the middle, then **Read aloud**. It reads from where you are, highlights the paragraph it's reading, and carries on into the next chapter. The bar at the bottom has pause, speed and a **sleep timer** (10 minutes to 1 hour, or end of chapter). If nothing plays, check your phone has a text-to-speech voice: search "text-to-speech" in your phone's Settings.

### Highlights and notes

Press and hold on some words to select them, then tap **Highlight** or **Add note**. Tap a highlight later to read or change its note, remove it, or make a quote card. All your highlights are collected under **Me**, and the ones for the current fic are under **Chapters**.

### Quote cards

Tap a highlight, then **Make a quote card**. You get a pretty picture of the quote with the fic's title and author, in four styles (Dark, Light, Rose, Warm). **Share or save** opens your phone's share menu, so you can send it to a friend or save it to Photos or Drive.

### PDFs with special fonts

Authors sometimes use a different font for text messages, signs or letters. FicNook spots these in PDFs and shows them in a different font too (typewriter-style for text messages, a clean sans-serif for others). It also keeps *italics* and **bold**. PDFs are made for printing, so FicNook has to guess where paragraphs and chapters start. If a fic looks off, try the EPUB download instead.

## Me

- **Update now** appears at the top (here and in your Library) when a new version is ready. FicNook looks for new versions whenever you come back to it, and the **Check for updates** button (in the FicNook app box) checks right away.
- **Your reading this year**: fics finished, words read, time spent reading, and your best streak of days in a row. **See your recap** shows the whole year: busiest month, top fandoms, ships and tags, your longest fic, and more. You can look back at earlier years too.
- **Your reading goal**: pick a number of fics a week, month or year, or minutes a day, and watch the bar fill up.
- **Your shelves**: make new shelves (like "Comfort reads" or "Reread someday") or remove ones you don't need. Removing a shelf never removes the fics.
- **Reading settings**: same as **More** in the reader.
- **Highlights and notes**: everything you've highlighted. Tap one to jump to it.
- **Fic folder**: change the folder, or check it for new fics right now.
- **Back up your library**: see below.
- **Help**: short answers to common questions.

## Backups

Under **Me**, tap **Back up now**. Your phone asks where to save the backup file. Pick **Drive** to keep it in your Google Drive. The backup holds everything: your fics, shelves, favourites, ratings, notes, highlights, bookmarks, goal and stats.

To bring it back (on a new phone, for example), tap **Restore from a backup** and pick the file. Restoring adds to what's already there, so it never wipes your current library.

## Home screen widget

Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap **Widgets**, find **FicNook**, and drag the **Keep reading** widget out. It shows the fic you're reading and how much is left. Tap it to jump straight back in.

## Privacy

Your fics never leave your phone. FicNook reads each file on the phone and keeps your library there. The only time it goes online is to check GitHub for app updates. Backups only go where you choose to save them.

## The web version

There's also a web version of FicNook that runs in a browser. It has the same reader and library (read aloud uses your browser's voice, where it has one). It can't watch a fic folder, use the volume buttons, make backups, update itself or show a widget, but it can restore a backup made in the app.
