    Help

    How do I get a fic in here?
    1. On AO3, open the fic and tap Download near the top.
    2. Pick PDF (or EPUB, which tends to come out even cleaner).
    3. Come back here, tap Open a fic, and choose it from your Downloads or Files.
    How do I change the text size or colors?

    While reading, tap the Aa button at the top. You can pick light, sepia, dark or night mode, change the font and size, and tuck away author's notes. If the top bar is hidden, scroll up a little to bring it back.

    Will it remember where I stopped?

    Yes. Fic Nook saves your chapter and spot on this device as you read. Just tap the fic on your shelf to jump back in.

    Something looks wrong in a PDF

    PDFs are made for printing, so Fic Nook has to guess where paragraphs and chapters start. Italics are lost, and now and then a heading lands in the wrong spot. If a fic looks off, download the EPUB version from AO3 instead and open that.

    Your fics never leave your device. Fic Nook reads the file right here in your browser and keeps your shelf only on this phone or computer.
