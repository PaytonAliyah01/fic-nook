        Help

        How do I get a fic in here?

        On AO3, open the fic, tap Download and pick PDF or EPUB (EPUB comes out a little cleaner). If you chose a fic folder, it shows up by itself. Otherwise tap Add a fic in your Library and pick the file.

        A fic got new chapters. What do I do?

        Download it again from AO3. Fic Nook sees it's the same fic and updates the one in your Library, keeping your spot, highlights and shelf. The card says how many chapters are new.

        How do I use the reading screen?

        Tap the middle of the page to show or hide the controls. Tap the right or left edge, swipe, or press your volume buttons to turn pages. The controls have text size, theme, read aloud and chapters. Everything else is under More.

        How does read aloud work?

        Tap the middle of the page, then Read aloud. It reads from where you are and carries on into the next chapter. The sleep timer stops it after a set time or at the end of the chapter. If nothing plays, check that your phone has a text-to-speech voice (search "text-to-speech" in your phone's Settings).

        How do I highlight or add a note?

        Press and hold on some words to select them, then tap Highlight or Add note. Tap a highlight to read or change its note. Your highlights are listed here under Me, and under Chapters while reading.

        Shelves, ratings, notes and bookmarks

        Tap the ⋮ button on a fic to rate it, write private notes, or put it on your own shelves (make new shelves there or here under Me). While reading, tap the middle, then Bookmark to save a spot. Your bookmarks are under Chapters.

        Night light, auto-scroll and quote cards

        Under More while reading (or Reading settings here), turn on the Night light to tint the screen amber, or pick the Warm theme. Auto-scroll moves through the fic by itself at your reading speed. To make a quote card, tap a highlight and choose Make a quote card.

        Can I put Fic Nook on my home screen?

        Yes. Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, find Fic Nook and drag the Keep reading widget out. Tapping it opens the fic you were reading.

        Something looks wrong in a PDF

        PDFs are made for printing, so Fic Nook has to guess where paragraphs and chapters start. It keeps italics, bold and the author's special fonts (like text messages) when it can. If a fic still looks off, download the EPUB version from AO3 instead.
